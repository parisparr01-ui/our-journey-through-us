Happy Birthday, Ava ❤️

From February 14th to September 4th, you just traveled through our story.

Ava,

When I started making this, I didn't just want to give you something that said happy birthday. I wanted to give you something that showed you just how much has happened since the day we met.

Somehow, one moment at church camp turned into texting, praying together, dates, surprises, prom, birthdays, performances, graduations, trips, FaceTimes, time apart, coming back together, and so many memories in between.

Going back through every month reminded me that some of my favorite memories aren't necessarily the biggest ones. They're the little moments where I simply got to have you beside me.

We've had months where we were together constantly and months where there were hundreds of miles between us. But through every chapter, I'm thankful for the time God has given us and for every memory we've gotten to make.

And now we've reached September 4th.

Your birthday. 🎂

I hope today reminds you how loved, valued, and appreciated you are. I hope this next year of your life brings you closer to God, gives you countless reasons to smile, and becomes another year filled with memories you'll never forget.

These eight chapters are finished...

but our story isn't. ❤️

Happy Birthday, Ava-lynn.
